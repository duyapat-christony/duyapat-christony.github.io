**Today, I walked across the stage not only with a diploma in hand 🥳 but a heart full of gratitude 😊**

First and foremost, I want to express my deepest appreciation to my **family**. Your unwavering support and belief in me have been the driving force behind my achievements. You have been my inspiration throughout my educational journey. I couldn't have done it without your love and guidance.

To my **titos, titas, pinsans, and other relatives**, thank you also for the prayers, moral support, and the financial assistance you generously provided. *Salamat nunta ayat tan pananghihakit ni inpaay yun higak.*

I also extend my heartfelt gratitude to all my **teachers and mentors** from the various stages of my education. From my early years at [**Gumhang Elementary School**](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100066317156388), through [**Tinoc National High School**](https://www.facebook.com/TinocNHS305192) in junior high school, my time spent at [**Benguet State University - Secondary Laboratory School**](https://www.facebook.com/ctesls.bsu) during senior high school and of course my college studies at [**Benguet State University**](https://www.facebook.com/BenguetStateUniversity). I've been fortunate to learn from some incredible educators. Each one of you played a crucial role in shaping my knowledge and character. Thank you for becoming part of my educational journey.

Thank you also to the faculty, staff, and my former students at [**Tuba Central National High School**](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100070106051684) where I had my teaching internship. My experiences there helped me grow personally and develop professionally.

To the remarkable people I encountered at [**Sto. Niño Parish Convent**](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61555425196900) where I served as a convent boy/sacristan during my junior high school. Thank you for being part of my life and helping me build a strong spiritual foundation. Your unwavering support during my four years stay there has been truly remarkable and made a lasting impact on my life.

Thank you also to those who became my **friends and classmates**, this journey would not be fun without you all.

I also want to express my gratitude to [**DOST-SEI**](https://www.facebook.com/DOST.SEI) for the financial support you provided during my college studies. Your assistance eased the expenses of studying and allowed me to focus on my studies.

Above all, my heartfelt thanks to **God**, the source of all blessings, wisdom, and knowledge. **All the glory belongs to Him.**

As I celebrate this milestone in my life, I carry with me the love and support of all those who have been part of this journey. Your contributions have been invaluabe, and will always be remembered. 

**Thank you from the bottom of my heart 💝💝💝**

**PS: Thank you very much for the thoughful and generous graduation gifts I received. Hi Apo Dios i mangibukbuk ni bendisyon di pamilya yu, di salun-at tan paki ub ubla yu.**

To provide convenient access to the SF9 (Learner's Performance Report), I have created a dedicated online portal for students and parents. Through this secure platform, you can view a comprehensive breakdown of the learner's progress, which includes:

* *Term Grades:* Monitor academic performance across all learning areas.

* *Attendance Records:* Track the number of class days and days present each month.

* *Teacher's Remarks:* Read specific feedback and observations for each term.

For your convenience, the portal also allows you to download the report card as an image or print it directly. To ensure strict data privacy and security, each student accesses their individual report using their unique Learner Reference Number (LRN) alongside a personal passcode.

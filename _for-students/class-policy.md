>
**Hey students!** 🙋‍♂️<br>
I am your teacher Christony Duyapat. Welcome to our class website! This is a space to learn, share, and communicate, so let’s keep it positive and productive. Here’s what you need to know:
{: .prompt-info }

>
**Be Respectful**<br>
Treat everyone—classmates, teachers, and guests—with kindness.
Use friendly language in your comments.
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**Do Your Own Work**<br>
Make sure all your submissions are your own.
If you share something from another source, give credit!
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**Stay Safe Online**<br>
Don’t share personal info like address or phone number.
Be careful with what you post and who can see it.
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**Keep It Appropriate**<br>
Share content that relates to our class topics.
No offensive or disrespectful material allowed.
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**Join the Conversation**<br>
Check the website regularly for the latest announcements, deadlines, and learning materials.
Join in on the discussion by sharing your thoughts in the comment section!
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**Tech Troubles?**<br>
If you run into any issues, let me know right away.
Thanks for your patience while I sort things out!
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**Policy Changes**<br>
I might update this policy if needed. You’ll always be kept in the loop!
{: .prompt-warning }

>
**What Happens If You Break the Rules?**<br>
If you don’t follow these guidelines, you may receive a warning, and in some cases, your content may be removed. Let’s keep our space safe and enjoyable for everyone!
{: .prompt-danger }
